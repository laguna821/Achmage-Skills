[00:07] First Light for Planet Hunter ExTrA
[00:12] Planet-hunting astronomers have a new and powerful tool to search for potentially habitable worlds.
[00:21] ExTrA, just installed at ESO’s La Silla Observatory, uses three 0.6-m telescopes to look for tiny dips in the brightness​ of nearby red dwarf stars...
[00:30] ... caused by ​Earth-sized planets passing in front of their host stars.
[00:36] ​ExTrA focuses on red dwarfs because a transiting planet blocks out a relatively large proportion of light from such a small star.
[00:46] Red dwarfs are common in the Milky Way and are expected to host many Earth-sized planets.
[00:54] The novel design of ExTrA, together with the excellent conditions at La Silla, make it more sensitive than previous surveys.
[01:02] The atmospheres of planets found by ExTrA will be studied with the ELT — and may reveal the first signs of life beyond Earth!
