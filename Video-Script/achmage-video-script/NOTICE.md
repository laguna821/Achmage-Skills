## Research basis

The rules in `references/` come from the author's own analysis of on-screen text in 159 videos from 27 public channels (EBS 지식채널e, 역사채널e, 뉴스타파 5 Minutes, AJ+, NowThis, Brut, ESOcast Light and others). The skill describes patterns in the author's own words. It contains no video, image or audio from those channels and no transcripts of their episodes. Example lines in the references are short illustrations written for this skill, not transcripts of episodes. Channel names identify the sources of the analysis; the channels did not take part in this project and do not endorse it.

## No third-party media in the skill folder

The skill folder holds text, a Python script that uses the standard library only, and an HTML template. It loads nothing from the network. The example videos published next to the package (`../examples/`) contain third-party footage, music, sound effects and public-domain drawings; their sources and terms are listed in `../ASSET-NOTICE.md` and in each example's `credits.md`. Those terms apply to the examples, not to the skill.
