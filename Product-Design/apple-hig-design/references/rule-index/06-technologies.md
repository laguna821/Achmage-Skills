## AirPlay (`airplay`)

<https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/airplay>

### Best practices

- `airplay/best-practices/prefer-the-system-provided-media-player` **Do:** Use the built-in media player by default, and design a custom one only if it can't meet your app's needs. **Why:** It supplies standard controls and features, is easy to adopt, gives a familiar consistent playback experience, and fits most media apps. · P familiarity, simplicity · L interaction, platform · S compose, interact · M d/d/-/-
- `airplay/best-practices/provide-content-in-the-highest-possible` **Do:** Offer video in the full range of available resolutions in your streaming playlist so each screen gets a suitable one. **Why:** Without higher resolutions, content can look poor when streamed to a larger display, like 4K TVs. · P craft, flexibility · L attention, platform · S express, adapt · M d/d/t/t
- `airplay/best-practices/stream-only-the-content-people-expect` **Do:** Don't send app-internal media, such as background loops or short in-app clips, to an external screen. **Why:** Such content only makes sense inside the app itself. · P simplicity, familiarity · L value, platform · S interact · M t/t/-/-
- `airplay/best-practices/support-both-airplay-streaming-and-mirroring` **Do:** Offer both AirPlay streaming and mirroring. **Why:** Having both gives people the most flexibility. · P flexibility · L platform, interaction · S interact · M -/-/-/-
- `airplay/best-practices/support-remote-control-events` **Do:** Handle remote control events so people can play, pause, or fast forward from the lock screen, Siri, or HomePod. **Why:** People get playback control outside the app itself. · P flexibility, agency · L interaction, platform · S interact · M t/t/-/-
- `airplay/best-practices/dont-stop-playback-when-your-app` **Do:** Keep playback going when the app is backgrounded or the device locks, and avoid auto-mirroring in those cases. **Why:** People expect a show to continue while they do other things, and don't want other content streamed unless they choose it. · P agency, responsibility · L flow, trust · S interact · M d/t/-/-
- `airplay/best-practices/dont-interrupt-another-apps-playback-unless` **Do:** Unless your app begins immersive content, let other apps' ongoing playback continue; play launch or autoplay video only on the local device. **Why:** Interrupting audio people already chose disrupts them. · P agency, responsibility · L flow, trust · S interact · M d/t/-/-
- `airplay/best-practices/let-people-use-other-parts-of` **Do:** Keep the app usable while AirPlay runs, and make sure other videos don't start and cut off the stream when people leave the playback screen. **Why:** People shouldn't be locked on one screen or have their streaming disrupted. · P agency · L flow, interaction · S interact · M d/t/-/-
- `airplay/best-practices/if-necessary-provide-a-custom-interface` **Do:** If you build your own player, include AirPlay buttons matching the system ones in look and behavior, with distinct states for starting, playing, and unavailable, using only Apple symbols, placed at lower right. **Why:** Matching the system controls keeps AirPlay recognizable and intuitive. · P familiarity, craft · L interaction, platform · S interact, express · M d/t/-/-

## Always On (`always-on`)

<https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/always-on>

### Best practices

- `always-on/best-practices/hide-sensitive-information` **Do:** Redact personal details that people wouldn't want bystanders to see, such as bank balances or health data, including in notifications. **Why:** Casual observers could view private information on a display that stays on. · P responsibility · L trust · S include · M t/-/t/-
- `always-on/best-practices/keep-other-types-of-personal-information` **Do:** Where it fits, keep non-sensitive personal updates glanceable, like workout pace or a ride arriving; people can turn Always On off. **Why:** People appreciate quick timely updates during an activity, and they retain control over whether anything shows. · P purpose, agency · L value, attention · S define, include · M t/-/-/-
- `always-on/best-practices/keep-important-content-legible-and-dim` **Do:** Dim secondary text, images, and color fills in Always On so key information stands out; consider dropping rich images or big color areas in favor of dimmed colors. **Why:** Dimming nonessential content gives more prominence to what matters to people. · P simplicity, craft · L attention · S express, compose · M t/t/d/d
- `always-on/best-practices/maintain-a-consistent-layout` **Do:** Keep the layout stable entering, during, and leaving Always On: show interactive parts as unavailable instead of removing them, and make updates rare and subtle. **Why:** Needless changes distract, particularly on a face-up iPhone where screen motion is noticeable even when people aren't looking. · P simplicity, craft · L attention, flow · S interact, compose · M t/-/-/-
- `always-on/best-practices/gracefully-transition-motion-to-a-resting` **Do:** Let motion wind down smoothly into a resting state instead of cutting it off abruptly. **Why:** Finishing the motion smoothly signals the transition and keeps people from thinking something broke. · P craft, delight · L attention · S interact, express · M d/d/t/-

- `app-clips/designing-your-app-clip/allow-people-to-complete-a-task` **Do:** Let people finish the task, complete the demo, or beat the level inside the App Clip without needing to install the full app. **Why:** Forcing installation blocks people from the experience they came for. · P agency, purpose · L flow, value · S structure, define · M d/d/-/-
- `app-clips/designing-your-app-clip/focus-on-essential-features` **Do:** Include just what the task needs in an App Clip and leave advanced features to the full app; a demo should show core functionality. **Why:** App Clip interactions are brief and focused. · P simplicity, purpose · L value, attention · S define · M d/d/t/t
- `app-clips/designing-your-app-clip/dont-use-app-clips-solely-for` **Do:** Make App Clips genuinely useful for getting tasks done; don't use them to promote products or services, and don't show ads in them. **Why:** App Clips are supposed to deliver real value to people. · P purpose, responsibility · L value, trust · S define · M d/d/-/-
- `app-clips/designing-your-app-clip/avoid-using-web-views-in-your` **Do:** Build App Clips with native components; if only web content is possible, provide a quick link to your site instead of an App Clip. **Why:** Native components deliver an experience of app quality. · P craft, familiarity · L platform · S compose, adapt · M t/-/-/-
- `app-clips/designing-your-app-clip/design-a-linear-easy-to-use` **Do:** Keep the App Clip linear and focused: no tab bars, complex navigation, or settings, with minimal screens and entry forms and little extra information. **Why:** Reducing complexity suits a quick, single-purpose experience. · P simplicity · L flow, attention · S structure, compose · M d/d/t/t
- `app-clips/designing-your-app-clip/on-launch-show-the-most-relevant` **Do:** At launch, drop needless steps and take people straight to the App Clip section matching their context. **Why:** People get to what they need faster. · P agency, simplicity · L flow, value · S structure · M d/d/-/-
- `app-clips/designing-your-app-clip/ensure-people-can-use-your-app` **Do:** Make an App Clip instantly usable by bundling all needed assets, skipping splash screens, and never making people wait at launch. **Why:** Waiting at launch gets in the way of the quick task people came to do. · P agency, craft · L flow, interaction · S interact · M d/d/-/-
- `app-clips/designing-your-app-clip/ensure-your-app-clip-is-small` **Do:** Keep the App Clip lightweight by cutting unneeded code and unused assets, and avoid fetching extra data, especially when bandwidth is limited. **Why:** Less to load means a quicker launch and keeps the feeling of immediacy. · P craft, agency · L flow, interaction · S interact · M d/d/-/-
- `app-clips/designing-your-app-clip/make-the-app-clip-shareable` **Do:** Let people share links to specific points within the App Clip and encourage them to pass it along; shared links launch it from Messages. **Why:** Links make the App Clip reachable by recipients right from a conversation. · P flexibility, purpose · L flow, platform · S structure, interact · M t/d/-/-
- `app-clips/designing-your-app-clip/make-it-easy-to-pay-for` **Do:** Consider supporting Apple Pay so checkout is fast and shipping details need no typing. **Why:** Typing payment information is slow and prone to mistakes. · P simplicity, agency · L trust, flow · S interact · M d/d/-/-
- `app-clips/designing-your-app-clip/avoid-requiring-people-to-create-an` **Do:** Don't force account creation before value is delivered; skip it, or ask after the task is done, and if needed collect minimal info, for example via Sign in with Apple. **Why:** Account creation takes time and effort. · P agency, simplicity · L flow, trust · S structure, interact · M d/d/-/-
- `app-clips/designing-your-app-clip/provide-a-familiar-focused-experience-in` **Do:** Once the full app replaces the App Clip, give returning users a focused, familiar experience without extra steps such as logging in again. **Why:** Installed-app launches take over the App Clip's invocations, and added steps would slow people who were already using it. · P familiarity, agency · L flow · S structure, interact · M d/t/-/-

- `app-clips/printing-guidelines/if-you-create-large-batches-of` **Do:** For big batches, test the print workflow with small runs, print the URL and filename next to each code, keep a file mapping each SVG to its URL, and track versions carefully. **Why:** A code can't show which URL it encodes, so mix-ups are easy without records. · P craft, responsibility · L platform · S interact · M -/-/-/-

## Apple In-App Purchase (`apple-in-app-purchase`)

<https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/apple-in-app-purchase>

### Best practices

- `apple-in-app-purchase/best-practices/let-people-experience-your-app-before` **Do:** Let people try the app before buying; with auto-renewable subscriptions, consider limited free access to content. **Why:** Having enjoyed the app and seen its value, people are more willing to pay. · P agency, responsibility · L value, trust · S define, structure · M d/d/-/-
- `apple-in-app-purchase/best-practices/design-an-integrated-shopping-experience` **Do:** Make the store and its transactions look and feel like the rest of your app. **Why:** People shouldn't feel they have landed in a different app when browsing or buying. · P familiarity, craft · L attention, trust · S compose, express · M d/d/-/-
- `apple-in-app-purchase/best-practices/use-simple-succinct-product-names-and` **Do:** Give products short names that fit without truncating or wrapping, with plain, direct descriptions. **Why:** Clear, concise titles help people find products quickly. · P simplicity · L content · S write · M d/d/d/d
- `apple-in-app-purchase/best-practices/display-the-total-billing-price-for` **Do:** Show the full amount that will be billed for every purchase option, whatever its type. **Why:** People must know what they'll be charged for anything they consider buying. · P responsibility · L trust, content · S write, include · M d/d/t/t

- `apple-in-app-purchase/best-practices/use-the-default-confirmation-sheet` **Do:** Keep the system's purchase confirmation sheet exactly as is, without altering or imitating it. **Why:** It exists to help prevent accidental purchases. · P responsibility, familiarity · L trust, interaction · S interact · M t/t/-/-
