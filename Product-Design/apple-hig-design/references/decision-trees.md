# Decision trees

Use a tree when two or more components could do the job. Each tree starts from what the person needs, asks a few questions, and ends at a component (or at "no component") with the HIG rules that justify it. Each tree also lists the distinctions the HIG leaves open; treat those as [HIG none] and ask the user.
