# Platform profile: visionOS

**Key numbers (from HIG tables):**

- Text size: default 17 pt, minimum 12 pt
- Control size: default 60x60 pt, minimum 28x28 pt

## Platform traits

- **Space:** On Apple Vision Pro, use the limitless space for windows, volumes and 3D content, and offer deeply immersive experiences.
- **Immersion:** On visionOS, let people move fluidly between immersion levels, from the Shared Space with several apps to a Full Space with one.
- **Passthrough:** On visionOS, use passthrough so people see their surroundings alongside virtual content, with the Digital Crown adjusting the amount.
- **Spatial Audio:** On visionOS, use Spatial Audio, and with permission to read the surroundings you can tune it for custom experiences.
- **Eyes and hands:** On visionOS, support looking at an object and tapping indirectly, as well as touching items directly with a finger.
- **Ergonomics:** On visionOS, prioritize visual comfort, trusting the system to place content relative to the head.
- **Accessibility:** On Vision Pro, support assistive technologies and prefer system UI components, which include accessibility support by default.
