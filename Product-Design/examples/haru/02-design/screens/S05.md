Save and add another: Shift+Return on regular keeps the popover open with title cleared [assumption]; no separate button, to keep one prominent action. Compact has no second-add shortcut.

## Layout
- **Compact:** bottom sheet at full height of what it needs, grabber not needed because it does not resize `sheets/platform-considerations/ios-ipados/include-a-grabber-in-a-resizable`. Top row: 취소 (leading), title "새 할 일", 추가 (trailing). Title field on top; three pop-ups below, each a full-width row with a visible label. Keyboard opens at once; the sheet stays above it `virtual-keyboards/platform-considerations/ios-ipados/use-the-keyboard-layout-guide-to`. Return key reads 추가 `virtual-keyboards/best-practices/consider-customizing-the-return-key-type`.
- **Regular:** popover aimed at the 새 할 일 button, sized to content `popovers/best-practices/avoid-making-a-popover-too-big`; same order, 취소 and 추가 at the bottom of the popover trailing. Opening another popover (date picker) from inside is the pop-up's own menu, not a second popover: the date picker in D03 replaces the quick-add content inline rather than stacking `popovers/best-practices/show-one-popover-at-a-time`.
- Spacing: 12 px around controls, multiples of 4 px [House 11]; targets 44 px compact, 28 px regular.

## Lenses
**Attention:** the title field is the only focus on open; one prominent button; pop-ups are secondary. **Flow:** reached in one step from S01, S02, S03, S07; leaves with Cancel, Escape, outside click, or save; list position is kept `design-principles/flexibility/preserve-a-persons-context`; no stacked modals `sheets/best-practices/display-only-one-sheet-at-a`.

## Why-chain
Capture happens in the middle of other things → Agency, Simplicity → short scoped modal with defaults → Sheet (compact), popover (regular) → `modality/best-practices/present-content-modally-only-when-theres` `popovers/platform-considerations/ios-ipados/avoid-displaying-popovers-in-compact-views` → [HIG transfer]
