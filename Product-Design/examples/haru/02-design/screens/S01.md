## Layout
- **Compact:** single column, full width. Top: toolbar with large title, trailing 새 할 일 button. Below: list. Bottom: tab bar, always visible `tab-bars/best-practices/make-sure-the-tab-bar-is`. Undo message sits above the tab bar. Rows keep a 44 px touch target; checkbox target 44 px `accessibility/mobility/offer-sufficiently-sized-controls`.
- **Regular:** sidebar | list pane | detail pane (S04, or "할 일을 고르면 여기에 보여요" when none). Toolbar over the list pane holds title and 새 할 일. Selected row stays highlighted `split-views/best-practices/to-support-navigation-persistently-highlight-the`. Pointer target 28 px; hover state on rows. App content at most 1200 px [House].
- Grouping: sections separated by headers and space, not boxes `layout/visual-hierarchy/group-related-items-to-clearly-express`. Alignment: checkbox, title and meta on one leading edge `layout/visual-hierarchy/align-elements-to-make-them-easier`.
- Spacing: multiples of 4 px; 12 px gaps around bezeled controls, 24 px around unbezeled ones where they sit apart [House 11] `accessibility/mobility/consider-spacing-between-controls-as-important`.
- Text: row titles wrap rather than truncate at large text `layout/adaptability/be-prepared-for-text-size-changes`; project and date meta on a second line `lists-and-tables/content/keep-item-text-succinct-so-row`.

## Lenses
**Attention** (lenses.md): first item is the 오늘 section at the top and leading edge `layout/visual-hierarchy/order-content-by-relative-importance`; only one prominent button `buttons/style/in-general-use-a-button-that`; accent color kept for 새 할 일 and the checked state, not spread over rows `branding/best-practices/apply-your-apps-accent-color-judiciously`; overdue uses words, not alarm color `color/best-practices/avoid-using-the-same-color-to`; extra detail (done list) is on demand `layout/visual-hierarchy/use-progressive-disclosure-to-make-layouts`.
**Flow:** first screen is useful at once, no sign-in `managing-accounts/best-practices/delay-sign-in-for-as-long`; relaunch restores the section and the done-section state `launching/best-practices/restore-the-previous-state-when-your`; every change reversible (complete: tap again; delete: undo and S08) `design-principles/agency/help-people-recover-from-mistakes`; the selected task stays highlighted in the split view.

### Visual-order method (lenses.md "Reading visual order"). A hypothesis, not measurement
1. **Intended first item:** the first task under 오늘. Second: the 새 할 일 button. Third: 지난 할 일. `design-principles/purpose/create-value`
2. **Size and weight (planned for the visual stage):** screen title largest; section headers medium weight; task titles at body size with Medium or Semibold weight; project and assignee meta smaller and secondary. Order still holds at 200 percent because rows reflow. `typography/conveying-hierarchy/adjust-font-weight-size-and-color`
3. **Contrast and color:** accent color appears only on the toolbar button and the checked circle; overdue gets no color. So nothing competes with task titles. `branding/best-practices/apply-your-apps-accent-color-judiciously`
4. **Position and grouping:** 오늘 section starts at the top and leading edge; 지난 할 일 follows below a header; done list is last and collapsed. `layout/visual-hierarchy/order-content-by-relative-importance`
5. **Layers:** toolbar and tab bar (control layer) sit above the list (content layer); any glass look is only on these bars, never behind rows. `layout/visual-hierarchy/differentiate-controls-from-content`
6. **Motion:** only the row settling into the done section and the section expand; both optional. `motion/best-practices/make-motion-optional`
7. **Built order:** 1) title and first 오늘 rows, 2) accent button 새 할 일, 3) 지난 할 일 header, 4) tab bar or sidebar, 5) 오늘 끝낸 일.
8. **Compare with 1:** matches. One conflict to watch: the accent button (strong contrast) may be seen before the first task; fix by keeping the button small and in the toolbar, not by shrinking tasks. In the empty state the add field is intended first and is the only accent item. `design-principles/simplicity/establish-hierarchy`

## Why-chain (main layout choice)
Person opens the app to see today → Purpose, Simplicity → sections by importance in one list → List with sections, disclosure for done → `layout/visual-hierarchy/order-content-by-relative-importance` `design-principles/simplicity/establish-hierarchy` → [HIG transfer]
